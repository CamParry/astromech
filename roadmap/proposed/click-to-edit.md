# Click to edit on the site

Click a piece of content on the site and land on that field in the admin.
Builds on `roadmap/planned/live-preview.md` and
`roadmap/planned/admin-bar.md`, which already link a page to its entry. Raised
on 2026-10-03. **Target: after 1.0.**

## Prior art

- **Directus** visual editing (April 2025, "highly requested"), **Sanity's
  Presentation tool**, **Tina** and **Storyblok** in core.
- **Payload** sells it in Enterprise. "Astro with contextual editing" is
  Tina's second most-voted discussion.

## Open questions

- How does the site mark an element with its entry and field: a data attribute
  the author adds, or one `<Image>` and a rich-text renderer add for them?
- Does it edit in place or open the admin form?
