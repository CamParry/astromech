# No GraphQL, multi-site or memberships before 1.0

GraphQL would be a second query language to keep in step with permissions, when an Astro site calls Astromech in process and the typed client (`astromech/fetch`) serves everyone else. Multi-site and multi-tenant change every table's scope. Newsletters and memberships, built into Ghost, are products of their own that hosted services already provide.

**Rejected for 1.0:** GraphQL (Payload, Directus, Contentful); multi-site (WordPress Multisite, Craft); Ghost's newsletters and memberships.
